A student machine learning project, fall 2026

How far will a stock jump after earnings?

  1. When a company shares its earnings, its stock often jumps, up or down.
  2. The simple guess is its usual jump: the middle of its last 8 jumps.
  3. We trained a machine learning model, our model, to beat that simple guess.

On Nov 1 we locked in guesses for 480 companies. Then 138 of them reported, Nov 2 to 6.
